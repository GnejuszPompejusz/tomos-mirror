<p>
Tomos is an unofficial client for the Polish Librus Synergia platform, built with Flutter to bring clarity to grades, messages, attendance, homework, and school information for students and parents.
</p>

<p>
Tomos is not affiliated with Librus. It is actively developed and may contain bugs or visual differences between screens as features and design evolve.
</p>

<p><b>Features:</b></p>
<ul>
  <li><b>Unified Feed &amp; Calendar</b>: Weekly and daily schedule views, school events, holidays, teacher absences, and substitutions.</li>
  <li><b>Grades</b>: View numeric and descriptive grades with weights, skills, categories, and subject grouping.</li>
  <li><b>Attendance &amp; Justifications</b>: Attendance statistics with custom grouping rules and absence justifications.</li>
  <li><b>Homework &amp; Trips</b>: Track assignments, upcoming school trips, and school year milestones.</li>
  <li><b>Messaging</b>: Read, send, and organize messages with tags, drafts, and attachments.</li>
  <li><b>Accounts, Security &amp; Theming</b>: Multiple user accounts, encrypted credential storage, auto-login, and customizable themes.</li>
</ul>
